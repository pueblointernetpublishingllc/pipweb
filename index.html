About

Pueblo Internet Publishing LLC is a Colorado limited liability company founded by Brian Jensen, MBA. The company exists to ship useful things: tools that make records verifiable, systems that make audits boring, and consulting that keeps blockchain where it earns its keep.

The company's serious work is done under the Blockchain Integration Strategies name. Experiments and side projects are published here under their own banners so nobody confuses the two.
