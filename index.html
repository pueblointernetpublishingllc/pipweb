About

Pueblo Internet Publishing LLC is a software and web publishing company based in Pueblo, Colorado, founded by Brian Jensen, MBA. It serves businesses in Pueblo, across Colorado and nationwide. The company exists to ship useful things: tools that make records verifiable, systems that make audits boring, and consulting that keeps blockchain where it earns its keep.

The company's serious work is done under the Blockchain Integration Strategies name. Experiments and side projects are published here under their own banners so nobody confuses the two.

Outside the company, Brian keeps a personal project: The Partnered Translation, a fresh translation of the New Testament from the Greek text with translator's notes, produced by him as a human editor working with an AI model (Claude).
